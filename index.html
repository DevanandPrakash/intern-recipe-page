chicken fried rice

Cooking Time: 30 minutes

Servings: 2

Ingredients

  • 2 cups cooked rice
  • 200g chicken
  • 1 egg
  • 1 carrot
  • 1/2 cup green peas
  • 2 tablespoons soy sauce
  • 1 tablespoon cooking oil

    • Steps

    1. Cut the chicken and vegetables into small pieces.
    2. Heat cooking oil in a pan and cook the chicken until done.
    3. Add the carrot and green peas and cook for a few minutes.
    4. Add the cooked rice and mix everything together.
    5. Add the egg and soy sauce, then stir-fry everything well.
    6. Cook for a few more minutes and serve hot.
